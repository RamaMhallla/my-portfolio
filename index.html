About Me

I am Rama Mhalla, a Master’s student in Telecommunication Engineering at the University of Calabria, with a background in Information and Communication Technology Engineering. My academic and project work focuses on intelligent networked systems, edge–cloud architectures, and data-driven modeling for real-world applications.

I am particularly interested in learning-based decision mechanisms, scalable system modeling, and the practical integration of edge and cloud intelligence through research-oriented and collaborative projects. Most recently, I co-authored a short paper on Transformer-based molecular dynamics prediction, presented at HC@AIxIA 2026.

Green-Aware 5G-SDN Control for Flying Ad-Hoc Networks

Ongoing · 2026

An SDN control approach for flying ad-hoc networks (drone networks) in which forwarding decisions take into account each drone's energy budget and the QoS needs of the traffic, under a changing network topology. Developed in the Smart and Programmable Networks course at the University of Calabria.

SDN control for drone networks

  • QoS-aware traffic handling based on models of 5G QoS classes
  • NWDAF-inspired telemetry to drive adaptive control decisions
  • Emulated drone network with Containernet and a Ryu controller (OpenFlow 1.3)

Smart Health Monitoring System

Details View Code

A hybrid smart health monitoring system that enables real-time vital sign tracking using wearable sensors. The system integrates edge and cloud computing, a Flutter-based mobile application, MQTT-based IoT data acquisition, and AI models for heart disease prediction and X-ray–based pneumonia detection. Developed in the Distributed Systems and Cloud/Edge Computing course at the University of Calabria.

  • Real-time monitoring of heart rate, SpO₂, and temperature via wearable sensors
  • Automatic switching between local inference (Edge/TFLite) and cloud inference (AWS SageMaker)
  • Dual-mode AI prediction for heart disease risk and pneumonia detection
  • Cross-platform Flutter app with offline capability and local database support
  • Full-stack AWS integration using Cognito, Lambda, DynamoDB, and QuickSight
  • MQTT-based communication for reliable and lightweight IoT data exchange
  • Edge and cloud processing evaluated in EdgeCloudSim for 100–500 devices: service time, task failures, processing and network delay, and energy consumption

Secure Smart Lock: Authenticated IoT Communication

Details & Slides

A Bluetooth smart lock with authenticated, integrity-protected communication between a Flutter app and an ESP32, developed as a team project in the IoT Security course at the University of Calabria and tested against common network attacks.

Authenticated BLE communication
  • Encrypted and authenticated Bluetooth messages using AES-256 and HMAC
  • Cloud connectivity over MQTTS with a Flask credential server
  • Replay protection with server-side OTP and nonce validation
  • Tested against man-in-the-middle and replay attacks
  • Security analysis with Wireshark, Bettercap and Nmap
